Lectures

A public lecture for a general audience is scheduled for Friday at 5:00 p.m., followed by a reception at 6:30 p.m.

Proposed lecture topics include ethical questions about AI in mathematics, the Leiden Declaration and related guidance, graduate education, the mathematics behind large language models, and mathematical approaches to AI safety. Speakers and talk titles will be announced here.

Main discussion topics

The workshop focuses on practical steps that mathematics departments can take in response to AI. The opening panel will address AI and the future of mathematics. Participants will be invited to submit questions in advance, and the organizers will moderate the panels.

Hiring, tenure, and promotion

How should departments assess mathematical achievement when AI tools contribute to research? Which changes to current evaluation practices are most urgent?

Graduate education

How should graduate courses, research training, advising, and assessment change? What can departments do to put new guidance into practice?

Research practice and refereeing

How should mathematicians use, check, and disclose AI-assisted work? What standards should apply to authors and referees, and how might practices differ across pure and applied mathematics?
