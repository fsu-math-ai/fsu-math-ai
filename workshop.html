April 16–18, 2027 · provisional dates

Workshop

A Friday–Sunday workshop for approximately 80 participants, including about 20 invited speakers or discussion leaders.

The workshop will focus on practical steps that mathematics departments can take in response to AI. The main questions concern graduate education, mathematical research practices, and the evaluation of achievement in hiring, tenure, and promotion. Participants will be invited to submit questions in advance, and the organizers will moderate the panels.

We especially encourage faculty, graduate students, and department leaders from universities in the Southeast to attend. Lectures and panels are planned as shared sessions, with smaller working groups and breaks for informal discussion.

Lectures

A public lecture for a general audience is provisionally scheduled for Friday at 5:00 p.m., followed by a reception at 6:30 p.m.

Proposed lecture topics include ethical questions about AI in mathematics, the Leiden Declaration and related guidance, graduate education, the mathematics behind large language models, and mathematical approaches to AI safety. Speakers and talk titles will be announced here.

Main discussion topics

The opening panel will address AI and the future of mathematics. The workshop will focus on the following topics.

Hiring, tenure, and promotion

How should departments assess mathematical achievement when AI tools contribute to research? Which changes to current evaluation practices are most urgent?

Graduate education

How should graduate courses, research training, advising, and assessment change? What can departments do to put new guidance into practice?

Research practice and refereeing

How should mathematicians use, check, and disclose AI-assisted work? What standards should apply to authors and referees, and how might practices differ across pure and applied mathematics?

Graduate-student activities

Graduate-student activities will be announced here. Possible sessions include career advice and a discussion led by graduate students.
