Graduate summer school

A three-day summer school is planned following the workshop, with dates to be confirmed. It is intended for approximately 30 graduate students in mathematics, applied mathematics, statistics, computer science, and related fields.

Course structure. Each day includes one lecture in each of the three subjects below, together with worked examples, guided exercises or laboratories, and a discussion of research questions. Each subject receives three lectures in total.
Subject 1 · Three lectures

Formalization and Lean

Proof assistants, machine-checkable mathematics, autoformalization, formal libraries, and guided Lean exercises.

Subject 2 · Three lectures

Mathematics of frontier AI

Attention, optimization, high-dimensional structure, and reasoning models.

Subject 3 · Three lectures

Mathematical AI safety

Robustness, uncertainty, verification, interpretability, control, and related research questions.

Case study

Throughout the school, students will consider an AI system that proposes or checks mathematical arguments. Exercises and discussions will examine its formalization methods, the mathematics behind the model, and questions about its reliability.

Educational materials

We plan to make the syllabus, lecture slides or notes, Lean exercises, and example files available online. Recordings will be shared with presenters’ consent.

The organizers and instructors will determine the order and exact timing of sessions. View the common daily structure.
