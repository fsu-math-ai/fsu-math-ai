Registration

The workshop is provisionally planned for April 16–18, 2027. Summer-school dates, application details, and registration arrangements will be posted once confirmed.

Who should attend?

The workshop is planned for approximately 80 participants, including about 20 invited speakers or discussion leaders. The summer school is intended for approximately 30 graduate students in mathematics, applied mathematics, statistics, computer science, and related fields.

Faculty, graduate students, and department leaders from universities in the Southeast are especially encouraged to participate.
